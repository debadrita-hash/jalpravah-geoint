Dam-break and flash-flood simulation

What happens downstream if the dam fails?

JalPravah derives the dam, reservoir and breach from the DEM, then runs two independent physics engines on the same valley: DualSPHysics (smoothed particle hydrodynamics) and Deltares Delft3D-FLOW. Open either run, or compare them side by side.

The scenario

Reservoir at crest level (blue) behind a 55 m dam; the valley corridor both models simulate is highlighted. Drag to pan, scroll to zoom.

Saved data

Every input, mesh and output is kept on disk so any run can be reopened, re-analysed or loaded into GIS.
